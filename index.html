Who We Are

Serving Qatar with Distinction

Based in the heart of Doha, Qatar, Princess Lanka Cleaning & Hospitality Services has established itself as an elite provider of professional cleaning, sanitization, and hospitality solutions.

We bridge the gap between world-class Sri Lankan hospitality standards, professional diligence, and the dynamic cleaning requirements of Qatar's modern corporate towers and luxury residences. Our crew is meticulously trained in advanced cleaning techniques, eco-friendly chemical management, and high-end hospitality services.

Through a rigorous standard of team selection, background verification, premium training, and absolute customer-centric commitment, we ensure that your environment is not just clean, but pristine, glowing with the signature luxury of the Princess Lanka brand.

Our Expertise

Premium Luxury Solutions

Commercial Office Cleaning

Routine janitorial services, office desk disinfection, common area maintenance, and floor care tailored for high-end corporate towers and business hubs.

Residential Housekeeping
