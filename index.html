Who We Are

Serving Qatar with Distinction

Based in the heart of Doha, Qatar, Princess Lanka Trading & Hospitality Service has established itself as an elite partner for commercial, corporate, and residential solutions.

We bridge the gap between world-class Sri Lankan hospitality, professional diligence, and the dynamic requirements of Qatar's rapid growth. Our services span premium hospitality, multi-sector trading, industrial cleaning, facility management, logistics support, and manpower solutions.

Through a rigorous standard of selection, premium training modules, and absolute customer-centric commitment, we ensure that every partnership glows with the signature luxury and reliability of the Princess Lanka brand.
